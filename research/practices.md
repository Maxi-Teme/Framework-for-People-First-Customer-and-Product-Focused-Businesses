# Practice index

Every practice from every company comparison, in one list, so practices on the same question can be compared across companies before a decision. Details live in each company's `comparison.md`; decisions live in `decisions.md`.

Area codes and fit values: see `README.md`.

**Outcome**: `to decide` until the linked topic is decided, then the outcome from `decisions.md` (`adopt` · `adapt` · `reject` · `park`). `note only` = no decision needed.

**Signal** (first read, not a decision): `adopt?` · `adapt?` · `reject?` · `park?` · `recheck?` · blank

`recheck?` (added 2026-11-23): the first read marked this `reject?` mainly because it differs from the draft framework, not because it harms customers or the product. It depends on the new hypothesis topics FD-10 to FD-13. Rejections that stay are ones where the practice caused harm (concealment, broken promises, pressure on workers).
